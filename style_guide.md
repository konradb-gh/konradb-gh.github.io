# Writing Style Guide — Konrad Boguszewski

Target voice (confirmed): first-person, looser, opinionated — see the semiconductors sample below. Your bachelor's thesis (*"The impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on small businesses in Poland: The case of Kompresor Centrum"*, Gdańsk University of Technology) is the only confirmed sample of your formal writing; it's kept below as a reference register for anything that needs to read as academic/formal rather than as your default voice.

## Voice & register (default — use this unless a piece calls for formal)
- First person, conversational but substantive — not academic, not fluffy
- Willing to state a plain opinion ("that's not a resilient system") rather than only hedging
- Uses a short, blunt sentence to land a point after a longer explanatory one
- Comfortable with a mid-paragraph aside set off by dashes
- Data points are used to back an opinion, not just described — the analytical instinct from the thesis carries over, the hedging doesn't

## Sample of your own confirmed voice
> "I've been thinking a lot about how fragile the semiconductor supply chain actually is. A handful of companies — TSMC, Samsung, a few others — make almost all the advanced chips in the world. That's not a resilient system. It's a single point of failure with a few backup switches, and we found that out the hard way in 2021."

Full piece: `semiconductors_sample.md`.

## Formal register (fallback, for academic/thesis-style contexts only)
- Third person, evidence-led, claim → data → interpretive close
- Heavy hedging: "it is important to note that…", "it is possible to conclude…", "as a result," "nonetheless,"
- Sample: "According to the data presented in table 4.1, it is safe to conclude that the pandemic had a negative influence on the number of sales in Kompresor Centrum."

## Banned / AI-sounding phrases
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Delve into"
- "Navigate the landscape of…"
- "Unpack"
- "At the end of the day"
- "In the ever-evolving…"
- "Game-changer," "revolutionize," "unprecedented" used as throwaway hype
- "It's worth noting that…" (you already have your own hedges — don't stack a generic one on top)
