I've been meaning to start writing this down for a while now. Not because I think I have it all figured out — I don't — but because thinking in public forces a kind of rigor that thinking alone doesn't.

This is going to be a mix of things: markets, technology, the occasional rant about supply chains. I'll state opinions plainly instead of hedging every sentence into mush. If I'm wrong, I'd rather be wrong clearly than vague and unfalsifiable.

One housekeeping note: anything here that touches money is me thinking out loud, not a recommendation. See the footer on every page — I mean it.
